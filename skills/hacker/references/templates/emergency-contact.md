## Escalation Triggers

- unexpected service degradation
- access to sensitive data beyond allowed proof
- out-of-scope system encountered
- suspected third-party impact
- legal or compliance concern

## Stop Procedure

1. Stop active testing.
2. Preserve relevant logs and timestamps.
3. Notify the engagement lead and emergency contact.
4. Document what happened, affected target, and current state.
5. Resume only after explicit approval.
